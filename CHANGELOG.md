# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-14

- First release. Any `todo.*` list drawn as dark item cards with a colour bar, sorted
  alphabetically, completed items hidden
- Tap an item to mark it bought; it stays two seconds with *Undo* before it goes
- Hold an item to rename it, change its quantity or delete it
- The add field splits the quantity out of what you type — *2 kg arroz*, *arroz, 2 kg* and
  *arroz 2kg* all add *arroz* with *2 kg* as its description, which Bring! shows as the
  specification
- On Bring! lists a *Going shopping* button tells the family through Bring!, after a
  confirmation
- English and Portuguese, following the Home Assistant language
